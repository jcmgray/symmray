## Background

Abelian symmetries:

- [Tensor network decompositions in the presence of a global symmetry](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.2994)
- [Implementing global Abelian symmetries in projected entangled-pair state algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.3595)
- [Advances on Tensor Network Theory: Symmetries, Fermions, Entanglement, and Holography](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.6552)

Fermionic tensor networks:

- [Fermionic Projected Entangled Pair States](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.4667)
- [Fermionic multi-scale entanglement renormalization ansatz](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.3184)
- [Simulation of strongly correlated fermions in two spatial dimensions with fermionic Projected Entangled-Pair States](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.0646)
- [Fermionic Implementation of Projected Entangled Pair States Algorithm](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.2743)
- [Grassmann tensor network states and its renormalization for strongly correlated fermionic and bosonic states](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.2563)
- [Gradient optimization of fermionic projected entangled pair states on directed lattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.03657)
- [Fermionic tensor network methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14611)
